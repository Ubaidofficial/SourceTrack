Questions

Before you install anything.

Does this replace Google Analytics?

For most founders, yes — SourceTrack covers visitors, sessions, pages, devices, countries and conversion events. It is not built for exhaustive event exploration or raw analytics debugging. If you need those, keep GA4 alongside it.

How do you detect AI referrals?

By referrer domain. When someone clicks through from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot or Kagi, that referrer is recognised and kept as its own channel rather than collapsing into direct traffic. We cannot see the prompt someone typed, and we don't claim to.

Do I need a cookie banner?

SourceTrack uses a cookieless visitor identifier by default — a server-derived daily-rotating hash rather than localStorage or cookies. Your own legal obligations depend on your jurisdiction and what else runs on your site, so check with your counsel rather than taking a vendor's word for it.

What counts as revenue?

Conversion values arriving from Stripe, a webhook, the manual conversion API, or a Shopify order. If none of those are connected, revenue columns are hidden rather than showing zeros.

How long does setup take?

One script tag, then point your conversion events at a webhook. Attribution starts building from the first pageview; journeys get more useful as touchpoints accumulate over the following days.

## 4. KI-109 — entitlement defect, found and fixed

**VERIFIED.** `www.techrupt.pk` was **sold 1,000,000** pageviews and **metered at 150,000** — 6.7× under, on a live paid site. Free sites sold 10,000, metered 5,000.

**Root cause:** #428 repriced `PLAN_DEFAULT_PV_LIMIT` and shipped **no backfill**. `getPvLimit` returns the per-site override whenever present, and `sites.pv_limit` carries `DEFAULT 5000` — so an override **always** exists and the defaults are dead code for every live row.

⚠️ **Recurrence is not fixed.** The column `DEFAULT` means the next repricing drifts identically. **Recommended: `DEFAULT NULL`, with `pvLimitFromPrice` also writing NULL** — otherwise the next webhook re-freezes the value.

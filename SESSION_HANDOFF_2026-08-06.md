## 7. Method notes worth carrying

- **Verify branch state by CONTENT, never by ancestry.** Squash merges defeat `merge-base --is-ancestor`; this produced a false "not merged" read twice this session.
- **Positive control on every check.** Rebuilding unchanged source twice establishes the noise floor before a diff is called a signal — that is what proved the a3 "style leaks" were per-build random element IDs. A grep that cannot demonstrate it finds anything proves nothing.
- **Translucent backgrounds break carried-forward contrast numbers.** A pair's ratio changes when the surface behind it changes, even though its own CSS does not. TrustBar's badge went 10.54 → 1.22 on a section flip. **The nearest OPAQUE ancestor is the background that counts** — compositing against the wrong one produced a wrong diagnosis in 2c Step 0 (reported cells at 2.00; they were fine at 6.68, and the real failure was the lede at 1.05).
- **A checker that reports "unresolved" is a false-pass surface**, not a neutral outcome.
